# Report artwork

Edit `index.html` and `reports.css`, then run `make gallery` from the repository root. Each report is 1200 × 800 CSS pixels, rendered at 2×. The visible reports have no sequence numbers; numeric filenames are stable asset identifiers.

## Amazon assets

- `amazon-logo.png`: official Amazon wordmark, from [Amazon’s press collection](https://press.aboutamazon.com/logos?p=4). [Original asset](https://assets.aboutamazon.com/2e/d7/ac71f1f344c39f8949f48fc89e71/amazon-logo-squid-ink-smile-orange.png).
- `amazon-products.png`: illustrative product imagery generated with the built-in image-generation tool. This is a transparent 1536 × 1024 contact sheet, with three columns and two rows. CSS selects each cell; all product names, amounts, categories, and connector lines are editable HTML/CSS/SVG. These are illustrative products, not actual order photographs.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Create ONE transparent-background catalog contact sheet asset for a beautifully art-directed Amazon household spending infographic. Canvas landscape 1536 by 1024, exact 3-column by 2-row equal grid, each invisible cell 512 by 512. Six individual studio-photographed product cutouts, one product group centered in each cell, with generous transparent margins, all objects fully inside their own cell. Top left: premium charcoal over-ear wireless headphones, upright three-quarter angle. Top center: sculptural cobalt blue metal desk lamp, rounded dome shade, slim stem and round base. Top right: three nested light walnut wooden drawer organizer trays arranged as one compact group, visible compartments. Bottom left: three small cream kraft coffee bean bags grouped together, understated blue rectangular label, no readable text. Bottom center: pale ivory daily moisturizer pump bottle with a muted coral cap and subtle blue label, no readable text. Bottom right: a hardcover cookbook standing at a slight angle, cream cover with abstract tomato-red vegetable shapes and navy spine, no readable text. Cohesive contemporary editorial product photography, premium natural materials, crisp edges, realistic subtle soft contact shadows contained close to each object, clean studio light from upper left. The objects should be clear and elegant at thumbnail size, fill about 70 percent of cell height and at most 75 percent of cell width. NO grid lines, NO boxes, NO background scene, NO price text, NO captions, NO brand logos, NO decorative blobs, NO other objects. Genuine alpha transparency everywhere outside the products and their short soft shadows.
