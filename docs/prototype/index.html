fin is a direct line from Plaid to your machine. A small, open-source tool with clear boundaries you can inspect yourself.

Read the source ↗
01

It can read. It can’t move money.

Balances, transactions, and investments. fin never requests account and routing numbers or identity data.

02

Your secrets have a home. Your Keychain.

Plaid keys and bank access tokens stay in macOS Keychain. Never in files, command-line arguments, or logs.

03

No fin account. No fin cloud.

No server to run and no telemetry. fin talks directly to Plaid from your laptop. You own the connection.

When you use an AI agent, the data it reads is also subject to that agent’s privacy settings.
