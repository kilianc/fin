A CLI for your financial
data, built for the AI era.

fin connects your banks, cards and brokerages through Plaid. It runs locally on your laptop, keeps its keys in your Keychain, and costs nothing on Plaid's free Trial plan. Ask your agent, query your transactions with SQL, or keep them in a Google Sheet.

                  sum(amount) filter (month(date) = 8) as aug,
                  sum(amount) filter (month(date) = 9) as sep,
                  round(100 * (sep - aug) / aug) as pct
           from transactions
           where amount > 0
             and date between '2026-08-01' and '2026-09-30'

Local and yours.

reads
Balances, transactions, holdings with tax lots, investment activity.
never requests
Plaid Auth or Identity: no account numbers, no personal details. Cannot move money.
secrets
macOS Keychain, service fin. State in ~/.config/fin/state.json, mode 0600.
data
Transactions and balances in a DuckDB file in ~/.local/share/fin; query it with fin sql. fin sheet copies them to a spreadsheet in your Google Drive, the only file there fin can see.
amazon
Experimental and off until you run fin amazon login: it reads your Amazon payments and order pages with your Chrome sign-in, so each charge opens into its items.
servers
None. fin calls Plaid's API from your laptop and prints the answer.
cost
Free on Plaid's Trial plan, up to 10 institutions. MIT licensed.
