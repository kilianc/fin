Open source · Read-only · Runs on your Mac

Your bank and brokerage data, on your own machine.

fin connects to your accounts through Plaid and answers you and your AI agents from your laptop. Your keys stay in your macOS Keychain, and you own all of it end to end.

go install github.com/kilianc/fin/cmd/fin@latest

Needs a Mac and Go 1.26 or newer. Then run fin init.

What you can ask

Ask in plain words. Your agent runs fin and writes the report. These numbers are made up.

Built to be trusted

Your financial data never passes through anyone but Plaid and you.

Read-only

Balances, transactions and investments. fin never asks Plaid for account numbers or identity, and cannot move money.

Local

No server, nothing to deploy. Keys live in your Keychain, state in one file only you can read.

Made for agents

Tables in your terminal, JSON for your agent, clear exit codes, and errors that say what to run next.

Free

Plaid's Trial plan connects up to 10 institutions at no cost. fin is MIT licensed.
