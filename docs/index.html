fin

Your bank and brokerage data, on your own machine, for you and your AI agents.

A local, read-only tool: your financial data goes from Plaid straight to your Mac, your keys stay in your macOS Keychain, and you own all of it end to end.

Your agent does the clicking. You only type passwords, accept terms and sign in to your banks.

Install

go install github.com/kilianc/fin/cmd/fin@latest

Needs a Mac and Go 1.26 or newer. Then run fin init.

Built to be trusted

Read-only

Balances, transactions and investments. fin never asks Plaid for account numbers or identity, and can't move money.

Local

No server, nothing to deploy. Keys live in your Keychain, state in one file only you can read.

Made for agents

Tables in your terminal, JSON for your agent, with clear exit codes and errors that say what to run next.

Free

Plaid's Trial plan connects up to 10 institutions at no cost. fin is MIT licensed.
