fin reads your bank, card and brokerage accounts through Plaid and keeps the data on your Mac, for you and for the AI agents you already use.

It's a small command-line tool. In a terminal it prints tables; when Claude or Codex runs it, it prints JSON. There's no server and no account with anyone but Plaid, and it can't move money.

or go install github.com/kilianc/fin/cmd/fin@latest

examples

You ask in plain words; the agent runs fin and writes the report. Each "try it" link opens the prompt in Claude Code or Codex. The numbers in these reports are made up.

details

What it reads
Balances, transactions, holdings with tax lots, and investment activity, through Plaid's Transactions and Investments products.
What it won't do
It never requests Plaid's Auth or Identity products, so it never sees account and routing numbers or your personal details. It has no way to move money, and it never deletes a connection.
Where things live
Plaid keys and access tokens in your macOS Keychain under the service fin. Everything else in ~/.config/fin/state.json, readable only by you.
What it costs
Nothing. Plaid's free Trial plan connects up to 10 institutions; fin items shows how many you've used. fin is MIT licensed.
What you need
A Mac, Go 1.26 or newer, and about fifteen minutes, most of it signing in to your banks.

commands

  • fin initWhat's set up, what's next, and a prompt for your agent
  • fin linkConnect a bank, card or brokerage through Plaid Link
  • fin accountsBalances for every account
  • fin transactionsBank and card transactions, newest first
  • fin holdingsPositions with cost basis and tax lots
  • fin investmentsBuys, sells, dividends and fees
  • fin itemsConnection health, consent expiry, slots used

Add --json for scripts. The README has the rest.
