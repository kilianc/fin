fin ~ local · read-only github ↗

Your financial data for your AI agent. Read-only, local, free.

fin connects your banks, cards and brokerages through Plaid and can't move a dollar. It runs on your laptop with no fin server in between, keeps its keys in your Keychain, and costs nothing on Plaid's free Trial plan.

Read-only, local, yours.

readsBalances, transactions, holdings with tax lots, investment activity. never requestsPlaid Auth or Identity: no account numbers, no personal details. Cannot move money. secretsmacOS Keychain, service "fin". State in ~/.config/fin/state.json, mode 0600. serversNone. fin calls Plaid's API from your laptop and prints the answer. costFree on Plaid's Trial plan, up to 10 institutions. MIT licensed.
